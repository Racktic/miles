Note on strategy: each potion's effect is hidden and can either INCREASE or DECREASE a stone's reward — the only way to learn it is to try. You have a budget of 20 turns per trial; aim to BOTH explore broadly AND earn high reward. Use the early trials to experiment — place stones into potions you haven't tested yet and observe the Outcome stone — since broad exploration uncovers more of the environment's hidden rules and builds up knowledge that pays off in LATER trials. Then exploit what you learned: transforming a low-reward stone into a high-reward one with the right potion scores far more than just submitting the stones you start with, so don't stop early while turns and untested potions remain.
